# music

A music player in two parts: `musicd`, a daemon that ships beside the plugin's `plugin.so` and plays outside the launcher, so music goes on while the launcher exits for a game, and the plugin, its front end, which talks to it over a Unix socket (`/tmp/mosaic-music.sock`; its pid is in `/tmp/mosaic-music.pid`). The plugin starts it by its path, in a session of its own, the first time something is played; it logs to `.userdata/<platform>/logs/music.txt`, and exits by itself after 10 minutes stopped. It plays MP3, FLAC and WAV (mono or stereo) through SDL's audio, decoded with the dr_libs decoders in `vendor/dr_libs`, from the `folder` setting's folder on the card (`Music` by default) and its folders. The player screen opens from a Music tile on the quick menu (the `tile` setting) or a game list button (the `button` setting, none by default), or `action:music.open`: it lists the folder's folders, then its tracks, in name order with numbers by value, and what's playing with its time. A plays a track and then the rest of its folder, or opens a folder; B goes back up, and closes it from the music folder; X pauses or plays on; L1 and R1 go to the previous track (or the track's start after 3 seconds) and the next; L2 and R2 seek 10 seconds; SELECT turns shuffle on or off and START steps repeat through off, all and one, both kept as the `shuffle` and `repeat` settings. Y opens the lock screen (also `action:music.lock`): the clock and the track, dim on black, taking every button but power until SELECT and then START within a second. `action:music.toggle` pauses or plays on from an FN button. Over the game list, quick menu, switcher and other plugin screens a now playing pill shows at the bottom for the `overlay` setting's seconds after a track starts or plays on (5 by default; `off` or `always`). While music plays it holds off auto-sleep (the `awake` setting, on by default). Before a game or pak launches the music pauses and the daemon closes the audio device, so the game can have it, and plays on when the launcher comes back; the `games` setting's `play` keeps it playing through games instead, where the device's audio allows two players. A sleep pauses it (it doesn't play on after waking), a pause closes the device after 10 seconds, and a power-off quits the daemon, by the socket or else by its pid. The daemon saves the queue (with its shuffled order), the track, its place and repeat in `.userdata/<platform>/launcher/music/resume.txt` when it quits or is stopped by its pid, and when a track is played, paused, held for a game or put to sleep, through a temporary file it syncs and renames, and removes it when the music stops at the queue's end; it keeps the file, not the plugin, as it has the queue and is what runs when the device powers off from a game. At the next start with no daemon running, the plugin shows that track paused at its place, on the pill and the player, without starting the daemon; the queue's tracks no longer on the card are dropped, and nothing is shown when the track itself is gone. X or `action:music.toggle` starts the daemon and plays on from there, with the `shuffle` and `repeat` settings as they are then. The `resume` setting (on by default) turns this off. It reads file names, not tags. From Bramley and MaxUI.
