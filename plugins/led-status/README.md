# led-status

The LEDs show the battery level or a theme colour, while the launcher is on screen. With the `mode` setting at `battery` (the default) it reads the battery every 5 seconds and lights every LED red at or below the `low_at` level (20% by default), yellow at or below `mid_at` (50% by default), and green above; with `low_at` at or above `mid_at` there's no yellow. A reading of 0 or outside 0 to 100 keeps the colour it had, and a colour is left for a higher one only once the battery is 2 points past its level, so readings around a level don't flicker. With `mode` at `theme`, the LEDs take the stock theme colour the `theme_color` setting names: `main`, `accent` (the default), `accent2`, `list_text`, `list_text_selected`, `hint` or `background`. Each LED keeps the brightness the user's LED settings give it. Stock's charging, critical and low battery, mute and sleep lights still take priority, and the plugin's colour comes back after them. A game sets the LEDs its own way, and turning the plugin off gives the user's own LED settings back. When another plugin's LED colour is on, it logs that once and tries again only when its colour changes. On a platform without LEDs, such as desktop, or a launcher without LED colours, it loads but logs that it's unsupported and does nothing, and its name on the Plugins page says so. From ProClifo and DucLQ92.
