# bt-reconnect

Reconnects the last Bluetooth device at start and after sleep. Stock doesn't record which device was last connected, so while the launcher is on screen it saves the connected device's address in the `last` setting whenever it changes. At start, which includes coming back from a game, and after the device wakes, with Bluetooth on and a saved device, it waits 8 seconds for the launcher's first look at what's connected. If nothing is connected by then, it asks the launcher to connect the saved device, which the launcher stops after 15 seconds. A try that hasn't connected 20 seconds after asking failed, and it tries up to 3 times, waiting 10 seconds before the second try and 20 before the third, so it gives up about 100 seconds in. A device connecting, any device, ends it, as does Bluetooth turning off; it never tries while Bluetooth is off, and doesn't turn it on. Sleeping in the middle of it stops it too. The `on_start` and `on_resume` settings (both on by default) turn each part off, and with both off it does nothing, not even saving the device. Clearing the `last` setting forgets the device, until one connects again; an address can also be typed there. The launcher runs the connect, as `bluetoothctl connect`; the plugin never runs a program. On a platform without Bluetooth, such as desktop, or a launcher without the Bluetooth calls, it loads but logs that it's unsupported and does nothing, and its name on the Plugins page says so. From dalexanco.
