summary=Warns and powers off cleanly on a low battery and after a long sleep, and shows the battery from its voltage.
description=Warns when the battery runs low and powers the device off cleanly, can power it off after a long sleep, and can show the battery worked out from its voltage.
# warns, then powers off cleanly, when the battery runs low
setting.guard=toggle|Low battery guard|on
setting.warn_at=choice|Low battery warning at (%)|5|3,5,10,15
setting.off_at=choice|Low battery power off at (%)|2|1,2,3,5
# the guard goes by the battery's voltage, along the device's curve, in place of stock's percentage
setting.guard_volts=toggle|Low battery guard uses the voltage|off
# shows the battery from its voltage beside stock's percentage: as a percentage, or in volts
setting.volts=choice|Battery from its voltage|off|off,percent,volts
# powers off when the device has been asleep this long, by waking it with the RTC's wake alarm;
# off until it's been checked on a device
setting.sleep_off=toggle|Power off after a long sleep|off
setting.sleep_hours=choice|Hours asleep before powering off|4|1,2,4,8,12,24
