# hold-lock

Locks every button the launcher sees, on the game list, the game switcher, the quick menu and the dialogs, with a padlock in the header beside the status pill while it's locked, in the theme's accent with its hint colour. Holding the `chord` setting's buttons (SELECT and START by default, or L1 and R1, L2 and R2, or `off`) for the `hold` setting's seconds (1 by default) locks, and holding them again unlocks; buttons still held from the unlock are taken until they're let go, so the chord's own releases don't reach the screen. With the `switch` setting on (off by default), the hold switch on the side of the tg5040 and tg5050 locks while it's on, and the chord can't unlock it until it's switched off. It's stock's mute switch, so it still mutes as stock's Mute settings say. On the h700 and desktop, which have no switch, the plugin logs that once and only the chord locks. A long power press still powers off, but a short one doesn't sleep. The volume keys, and the brightness with MENU held, are changed by stock's keymon outside the launcher, so they can't be locked, and the Launcher pak's settings screen and games aren't locked either. The lock isn't kept when the launcher restarts. From westo27 and Bramley.
