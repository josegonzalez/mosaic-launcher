LIBBET AND THE MAGIC FLOOR
Sample text manual for the screenshots

This is a sample text manual, made for the manuals plugin's screenshots. A text manual is a plain .txt file in the .manuals folder beside the game, named after the game.

Long lines are wrapped to the screen in the launcher's font. Up and down scroll a line at a time, and L1 and R1 a screen at a time.

The manual opens where it was left the last time.

Blank lines are kept, so a guide's paragraphs and lists keep their shape.
